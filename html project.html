About SmartNote

Your Personal AI Study Notes Manager

SmartNote is designed specifically for high school and university students who want a fast, distraction-free workspace to write lecture notes, organize subjects, and use AI to turn raw notes into actionable revision material, flashcards, and practice quizzes.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Create New Note Alt + N
  • Save Current Note Ctrl / Cmd + S
  • Search Notes Ctrl / Cmd + K
  • Bold / Italic / Underline Ctrl + B / I / U

Cloud Sync & Reliability

Your notes and custom subjects are automatically saved in real-time to your personal cloud workspace so they persist across page reloads. You can also export any note to PDF or TXT at any time.
